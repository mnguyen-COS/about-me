## *More thoughts about AI

I was extremely disappointed and disheartened with week 15's focus on generative AI usage. By asking AI to generate webpages, we are removing the initial human aspect of design, no matter how much we may tweak generated code afterwards to our tastes. Are we not creative enough to come up with our own designs? Designers in any industry, whether web, graphic, or others (interior, architectural, fashion, etc.) should be trusted to create their own interpretations of a style or aesthetic. 

In module 15.4, the prompt example given under "Use Case 1: Starting a New Component" reads "Create a contact form with fields for name, email, messsage, and a submit button... Style it with a modern clean design using a light gray background and blue submit button." This prompt assigns the task of creating a "modern, clean" design to AI when modernity and cleanliness are subjective qualities. The rise of AI "slop" demonstrates a visible decline in the quality of visual media in favor of quantity while taking away jobs from designers. 

Perhaps it could be argued that AI is a time-saver, but as demonstrated with my use of pre-existing templates found on Google, it is unnecessary to generate new content to take advantage of the convenience of pre-written HTML structures. Every prompt to an LLM is a brand new computation with environmental impact that outweighs that of a Google search. I aim to avoid generative AI as much as possible, and so I've tried to complete this lab without AI in a way that still keeps the spirit of the assignment.
